From Berlin to a new Nuri.

The name has history. The current product is a new chapter with a different team, architecture and ownership.

2014–2015

Bitwala begins in Berlin

The original Bitwala grew out of Berlin’s early Bitcoin scene.

2021

Bitwala becomes Nuri

The original company rebrands as Nuri and expands its consumer-finance ambitions.

2022

The old Nuri shuts down

The original Nuri files for insolvency; services are discontinued in December 2022.

2023

Bitwala returns

A new Bitwala company relaunches separately with former team members and acquired IP.

15 Jan 2025

The fork

Bitwala announces that the Nuri brand and nuri.com will move to new ownership and operate independently.

2025–2026

A new Nuri is built

Emin Mahrt and the new Nuri team rebuild the product around passkeys, self-custody, Bitcoin & Lightning, stablecoins and replaceable banking rails.

The fork matters: on 15 January 2025, Bitwala publicly announced that the Nuri brand and nuri.com would move to new ownership and operate independently.
