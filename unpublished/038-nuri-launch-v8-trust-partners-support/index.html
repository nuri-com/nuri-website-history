Simple outside. Deeper underneath.

Nuri’s public architecture describes passkey-based self-custody with co-signing and independent recovery. Advanced layers can include hardware security keys, physical passkeys, hardware-wallet cosigning and open recovery tooling.

Legal and regulatory boundary.

Nuri itself is not presented as a bank. The wallet is self-custodial software. Regulated services are provided by licensed partners.

  • Wirex Limited (UK) — authorised and regulated by the FCA as an Electronic Money Institution, FRN 902025.
  • Transact Payments Malta Limited — authorised and regulated by the Malta Financial Services Authority as an EMI for EEA card services.
  • Monerium hf. — authorised Electronic Money Institution supervised by the Financial Supervisory Authority of the Central Bank of Iceland.
  • ClickPesa Tanzania — licensed by the Bank of Tanzania under Payment System Licensing and Approval Regulations, licence NBPSL No. 0000-31.
