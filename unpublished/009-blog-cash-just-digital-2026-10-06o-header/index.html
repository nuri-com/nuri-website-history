Bitcoin turns 18

On 31 October 2026, Bitcoin turns 18. The whitepaper came out on that day in 2008. In Europe you are of legal age at 18. In the US, Bitcoin has to wait until 2029, when it turns 21. I think that fits. Bitcoin has grown up.

The title of the whitepaper was "A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Electronic cash. Money you can send directly from person to person, without a bank in between. Nuri did not invent this. Bitcoin promised it from day one. We want everyone to finally be able to use it.

Basically,Bitcoin was electronic cash from the start. After 18 years it has grown up. Now everyone has to be able to use it.

There are different kinds of money

Cash in your pocket belongs to you. You can hand it to someone, and nobody asks why. Nobody has to agree. Nobody is watching.

Money in your account belongs to you too. But someone else holds it for you. The bank can ask, freeze, refuse. Most of the time it does not. But it can.

On the internet, for a long time there was only the second kind. Every payment went through a bank or a payment provider. Bitcoin was the first form of real electronic cash. It turned the internet into something like a street, a marketplace. You can pay and get paid, across every border, without asking for permission.

But because most people count in euros and dollars, digital dollars and digital euros were added over the last years. Stablecoins. Bitcoin is global internet money. Euro and dollar are your local currency. You can hold both in your own wallet.

Basically,Cash in your pocket or money in an account. That is the whole question: who holds your money?

The shift from bank to wallet has begun

According to Crypto.com Research, around 774 million people own crypto today. In the last 12 months, more than 5.7 trillion dollars were sent and paid with Bitcoin and stablecoins. And according to Chainalysis, the value moving directly between private wallets grew by around 300 percent last year. Almost all of it in stablecoins.

I believe that in the next years fewer and fewer people will want to "cash out" their money. They will simply keep it as digital money in their pocket. A wallet, self-custody, control. That is their right. Like the right to free speech. The right to own cash and to pay freely, also across borders. In the past this was the money under the pillow, or the gold in your jewellery. Today it is a wallet.

Basically,Hundreds of millions of people already have a wallet. The right to own cash applies on the internet too.

Next to your bank, not instead of it

Nuri is not meant to replace Revolut. Or Wise, or your bank. Nuri is the digital cash you keep next to your account. It gives you something banks and exchanges cannot give you. Money that really belongs only to you.

If you want, you can add a Visa card, an IBAN or mobile money. That comes from our regulated partners, right now mainly Wirex. With it you can pay rent, withdraw cash at an ATM or send money home. It makes your digital cash more useful. But it is not the core.

We see the classic payment rails as a transition. A bit like a Trojan horse. They make the switch to self-custodied electronic cash easier. Nothing more.

Basically,Keep your Revolut. Nuri is the cash next to it. Card and IBAN come on top, if you want them.

What we promise

Nuri stores no KYC data. No name, no ID. To create a wallet, you only need a passkey and a username. If you add a card or IBAN, only Wirex verifies you. Never Nuri.

Only you can access your money. Untouchable, even for Nuri. Your passkey creates your wallet, with Face ID or a fingerprint. No password, no seed phrase. Backed up automatically in iCloud or Google.

Your money is yours, even if Nuri disappears. You can export your keys and use them in any other wallet. The recovery tool is public on GitHub and runs offline on your computer, without Nuri, without our servers.

Nuri is open source. The wallet code goes public on GitHub at launch, under the MIT licence. Anyone can check how it works.

Nuri does not ask who you are or where you come from. A browser is enough, wherever you are. There is also Nuri for iPhone and Android.

And we say one thing just as openly. Self-custodied money is not protected like a bank account. You look after it yourself, just like cash. That is the freedom and the responsibility of cash.

Basically,No KYC data at Nuri. Only you have access. Your money stays yours, even without us.

The old Nuri and the new Nuri

Many people who google Nuri find articles about an insolvency. That was the old Nuri, a crypto bank from Berlin. It used to be called Bitwala, founded in Berlin in 2014. On 19 May 2021, Bitwala became Nuri. It failed in 2022.

The problem was not the technology. The problem was who holds the money. The euro accounts were held at Solarisbank. Bitcoin and ether were held by Solaris Digital Assets. In the Bitcoin interest account, the Bitcoin was with Celsius. When Celsius shut down in June 2022, owners became creditors. 14,746 customers were affected. Everyone else could withdraw their money until 18 December 2022.

Bitwala exists again today, as a separate company. The old Nuri Vaults are supported by Bitwala, and that is a good thing. We like them.

The Nuri brand and nuri.com moved to new owners in 2025. Today's Nuri is a new company, with a new team and a completely different architecture. We are not a bank. We do not hold your money. That is exactly why the old failure no longer exists.

Basically,The old Nuri was a crypto bank. The new Nuri is a wallet. We do not hold your money.

Our story

We have seen how good this technology is. And we have seen how often it goes wrong when someone else holds the money. Exchanges that go bust. Lenders that stop withdrawals. Banks that close accounts.

An AI once called us "the patient pioneers". I think that fits. We waited a long time until self-custody could really be simple. With passkeys, it is now.

Today we are four people. I am Emin Mahrt, I founded Nuri. We have a few investors, but not many.

Our goal is very simple. Make self-custody so simple that everyone can use it. For people and for AI agents.

Basically,We have been building with Bitcoin in Berlin since 2012. Today we are four people.
