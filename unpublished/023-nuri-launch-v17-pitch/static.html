Your wallet.

Self-custody means Nuri does not hold your Bitcoin or stablecoins for you.

Passkeys make access simple. Multisig and recovery keep the deeper security underneath.

Read about self-custody →

Wallet first.

Cards, banks and local payment providers are services around the wallet.

If Nuri or a connected provider disappears, your self-custodial wallet is designed to remain yours.

Read about recovery →

Freedom money.

We believe anyone should be free to hold money and send it directly to anyone else.

Bitcoin made peer-to-peer electronic cash possible. Nuri makes self-custody simple enough for everyday use.

Regulated rails.

Wirex provides card and banking infrastructure. Monerium provides regulated euro infrastructure. ClickPesa connects local payment and mobile-money rails.

These services have their own licences, eligibility and terms. They do not become the custody layer for the wallet.

Why Nuri.

Most products solve one part: Bitcoin custody, stablecoins, cards, banking or remittance.

Nuri combines self-custody, Bitcoin, stablecoins and local payment rails in one wallet.

Comparison set: Cash App, Bitkey, Muun, Blitz, Strike, Wallet of Satoshi, Bitrefill, Bitwala, AQUA and Phoenix.
