Berlin roots. A new Nuri.

The name has history. The current Nuri is a new company and a new product architecture built around self-custody.

Read the Nuri / Bitwala history →

2014–2015: Bitwala grows out of Berlin’s early Bitcoin scene.

2021: Bitwala becomes Nuri.

2022: the original Nuri shuts down after insolvency.

2023: Bitwala returns separately.

15 Jan 2025: Bitwala announces the Nuri brand and nuri.com will move to new ownership and operate independently.

2025–2026: the current Nuri team rebuilds the product around passkeys, Bitcoin & Lightning, stablecoins and replaceable local rails.
