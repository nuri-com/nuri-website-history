11 · Business modelFour money flows.
Business model

Nuri makes money when money moves.

  • 01 · Conversions — take rate on currency swaps.
  • 02 · Transactions — frontend fee on routed flows.
  • 03 · Solver / spread — liquidity provision and intent solving.
  • 04 · Partner share — card, on/off-ramp, payouts.
