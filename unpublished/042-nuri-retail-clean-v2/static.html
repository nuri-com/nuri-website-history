Your wallet. Not ours.

Self-custodial. Nuri does not hold your Bitcoin or stablecoins.
Passkey secured. No password and no seed phrase in normal use.
Built for exit. Your wallet is designed to remain recoverable without Nuri.
Open source. Wallet and recovery tooling can be inspected.
Read more about security and recovery

Nuri uses passkeys, multisig and independent recovery paths to remove the usual single point of failure. Optional banking, card and cash-out services are separate from the self-custodial wallet and may require identity verification.

A new Nuri.

The old Nuri shut down. The name came back with new ownership, a new team and a different model: wallet first, services second.

Read the Nuri history
2014 — Bitwala starts in Berlin.
2021 — Bitwala becomes Nuri.
2022 — The former Nuri company files for insolvency.
2023 — The original founders return under the Bitwala name.
2025 — The Nuri brand and nuri.com move to new ownership. Bitwala and Nuri become separate companies and products.
Today — The new Nuri is built around self-custody, Bitcoin & Lightning and replaceable local payment rails.
