Open free wallet

Regulated services, separate from the wallet.

Nuri connects the self-custodial wallet to regulated and specialist providers for card, banking, e-money and local payment services. These services have their own licences, eligibility and terms.

The important boundary: those providers do not become the custody layer for the self-custodial wallet.
